<paletteName>_<tileName>.png

for <paletteName>, there are some specific designations...
- placeholder (for tiles that need some visual representation, but have not been rendered by the artist(s) yet)
- indicator (for tiles that do not appear or will be replaced when loaded into the game)
other than the above two, palette names should be used to group tiles based on a shared aesthetic

NOTES
- Spritesheets will be created by the automator. You do not need to create your own.
- Everything between angle brackets <shouldBeCamelCase>
	- DO NOT use spaces in names!
	- ONLY use underscores where they are designated specifically in the naming convention!
- Files you would like to store in this folder but you don't want to be processed by asset automation should begin with a leading underscore.
